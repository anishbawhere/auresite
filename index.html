Founders

Najia & Izza

What started as a small idea between us slowly grew into something much bigger than we imagined. It became a way of celebrating the craftsmanship and heritage that have always made Pakistan special. 

For Najia, this journey is shaped by over fifteen years of designing garments with patience and purpose. For Izza, whose background is in interior design, it comes from an eye for balance, texture, and thoughtful detail. Together, our different paths found a shared vision: creating pieces that honour our heritage with quiet confidence. "Defined by detail, sewn in elegance" is more than a motto to us—it is the standard behind everything we create.

— N. Irfan, I. Shehryar
