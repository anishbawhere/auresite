Color Accuracy & Product Disclaimer

We strive to display product colors as accurately as possible. However, actual colors may vary slightly due to fabric dye lots, weather conditions during dyeing, studio lighting, and individual monitor or screen settings.

Handcrafted Fabrics

Hand-woven textiles naturally feature minor variations, including darker threads or weave irregularities. These are not defects, but inherent characteristics that showcase the raw beauty and authenticity of handcrafted items.
